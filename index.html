Student success · 2026

One idea. One student. A global stage.

Apple’s Swift Student Challenge celebrates original app playgrounds built with Swift and SwiftUI. In 2026, Apple selected 350 winners from 37 countries. One of them learned to code at Parul University.

Top 350Apple Swift Student Challenge · 2026
Praneel PandeyFirst-year B.Tech CSE student

No Mac. No Swift experience. Just a big idea.

Starting from scratch, Praneel learned through Apple’s curriculum and Parul’s Swift Coding Club. In the Apple Lab, he built Blink Break—an eye-movement-controlled game made with Swift and SwiftUI.

During the final month, he dedicated 10–12 hours each day to completing his project. In approximately five months, he went from his first line of Swift to a globally recognised project and an official Apple certificate.

The projectBlink Break — an eye-movement-controlled game created using Swift and SwiftUI.
